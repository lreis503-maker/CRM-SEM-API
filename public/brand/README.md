# Identidade visual do Vortex CRM

Arquivo usado pela interface e pelo ícone da aba: `vortex-symbol.png`.

O símbolo foi adaptado da imagem enviada pelo proprietário, com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen` em modo de edição. As formas e as cores marrom e cobre foram preservadas, e a parede e o texto foram removidos. O arquivo final tem fundo creme, sem transparência, para combinar com o espaço da logo na interface.

Prompt da edição final:

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: the most recent generated image of the isolated brown and copper Vortex emblem. Change ONLY the background: remove every checkerboard square, wall texture and speckle outside the emblem, and replace them with a perfectly flat uniform warm ivory color, exact #fff3e4 across the entire canvas. This is an app icon on an ivory tile. Keep the V-shaped emblem pixel-for-pixel as much as possible: same exact silhouette, proportions, brown left arm, copper segmented right arm, rounded edges, positions and internal material details. Do NOT redesign or add any letters, wordmark, shadows, gradients, wall, pattern or watermark. Keep centered on a square canvas with just 5% surrounding margin, clean sharp edges. The background MUST be completely solid #fff3e4, NO checkerboard and NO transparency simulation.
```
